Explore Varanasi

Varanasi is a historic city on the Ganga, known for its riverfront ghats, temples and nearby Buddhist heritage at Sarnath. This guide brings together selected highlights.

Check current temple visitor rules and ghat conditions locally. Keep riverfront areas clean and follow safety notices near the water.
