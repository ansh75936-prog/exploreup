# Homepage UI rules

- Keep the hero message short and destination-led.
- Search is the primary action; retain one search input.
- Show region discovery immediately below the hero.
- Featured districts use locally hosted photographs with 16:9-ish crops.
- The 75-district discovery list is one district per row, with a left thumbnail and right-side title/context.
- Keep all existing city-opening handlers, filters, and modal behavior.
- Do not remove original district images in homepage rendering.
